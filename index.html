FAQ.

Will my QR code expire?

No. These are static codes: the content is encoded directly in the pattern, so a code keeps working as long as its destination does.

Do the line and pixel patterns scan?

Yes, but stylized patterns have less margin for error. Keep strong contrast, use Quartile or High correction for print, and test with a few phones before a large run.

Can I use these codes commercially?

Yes. Use them on packaging, menus, signage, business cards and more.
